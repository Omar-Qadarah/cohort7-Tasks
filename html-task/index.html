Destination Overview

Tokyo, Japan's capital city, is a bustling metropolis that seamlessly blends the traditional with the modern. From historic temples to cutting-edge technology, Tokyo offers a unique experience for every visitor. Explore the vibrant neighborhoods, indulge in world-class cuisine, and immerse yourself in the rich culture of this dynamic city.

Tokyo, the capital of Japan, once known as Edo, is a city that seamlessly blends tradition and modernity. From historic temples to cutting-edge technology, Tokyo offers a unique experience for every visitor. Explore the bustling streets of Shibuya, enjoy the tranquility of the Meiji Shrine, and indulge in world-class cuisine at local izakayas.

best time to visit Tokyo is during the cherry blossom season in spring or the autumn foliage season.

Highlights

From the incense-filledhalls of Senso-ji Temple to the scramble of the Shibuya Crossing, and the stalls of Tsukiji Outer Market, Tokyo rewards travelers who like to wander. JR trains (the city's rail network) remain the fastest way to cross between districts. and the Tokyo Tower, a symbol of Japan's post-war rebirth, offers panoramic views of the cityscape.
